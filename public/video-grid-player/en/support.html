Support

System requirements

  • macOS 14 Sonoma or later
  • Apple Silicon (M1 or later) only. Intel Macs are not supported.

Supported formats

If a video plays in macOS's built-in QuickTime Player, it plays in this app. The App uses macOS's AVFoundation under the hood, so there are no extra codec packs to install.

What plays reliably

.mp4 and .mov files containing H.264 or HEVC video (the formats produced by iPhones, cameras, and most editing apps).

What may not play

VP8 and VP9 codecs (often found in videos downloaded from YouTube and similar sites) are not supported. AV1 plays only on Macs with AV1 hardware decoding (M3 or later). Note that even files with a .mp4 extension may not play if their internal video codec is VP8 or VP9. Some .mkv and .webm files are unsupported at the container level. Open the file in QuickTime Player first to confirm it plays.

Recognized extensions

mp4, m4v, mov, mpg, mpeg, m2ts, mts, ts, 3gp, 3g2, dv

Open videos with a URL

Start playback from Terminal, Shortcuts, or another app by passing files in a URL.

Format

videogridplayer://play?file=<absolute path>[&file=<absolute path>...]

Example (Terminal)

open "videogridplayer://play?file=/Users/you/Movies/a.mp4&file=/Users/you/Movies/b.mp4"

From Shortcuts

Put a URL in this form into a "URL" action and connect it to an "Open URLs" action.

Notes

  • Use absolute paths starting with /. Percent-encode spaces and similar characters (%20)
  • One URL opens up to 9 files. They go into empty slots in order; files that don't fit aren't opened
  • A URL doesn't change the layout. Switch it in the app first if needed
  • Only files inside folders you've already opened in the app (granted access to) can be opened
