Support

Requirements

  • macOS 26 or later / Apple Silicon (M1 or later) only. Intel Macs are not supported.
  • On the other Mac: turn on Screen Sharing in System Settings > General > Sharing.
  • For High Performance, UDP 5900–5902 must pass between the Macs. On VPNs or networks that block UDP, use Standard.

Getting started

  1. Click "Add connection" (+) below the list on the left.
  2. Enter user@host (for example koji@my-mac.local) and the login password of that Mac, then click "Add and connect".
  3. Press ⌃⌥1–9 to switch to the Mac in that position in the list.

On first launch the App asks for the "Local Network" permission. It is needed to reach Macs on your network.

Troubleshooting

"Wrong password"

Right-click the Mac in the list and choose "Enter password…".

High Performance does not connect, or no video arrives

Check the findings in the diagnostics window (⌃⌥D). On networks that block UDP, switch the Mac's "Video" setting to Standard from the right-click menu. Standard has no audio.

The picture glitched or froze

Press ⌃⌥R to refresh the picture (High Performance only). While the picture is frozen, a "Recovering video" notice may appear at the bottom right of the window.

⌘Q and similar keys do not work on your own Mac

The ⌘ key is being sent to the remote Mac. Press ⌃⌥0 to switch it back to your own Mac.

No sound

Standard mode has no audio. In High Performance, only the Mac you are looking at (the selected one, or the one in a separate window) plays sound.
