Dropbox video preview, built for real work.

Dropbox's web preview is handy, but comparing several clips at once or changing layouts on the fly still takes effort. This app is designed around three things: laying videos out, switching between them, and getting playback started instantly.

Dropbox Authorization Flow

Multi-Video Player uses secure OAuth 2.0 to connect to your Dropbox account. Your password is never shared with the app.

Step 1: Start login

Click the "Connect Dropbox" button in the app.

Step 2: Authorize on Dropbox

Review the requested permissions on Dropbox's official page and click "Allow".

Step 3: Start using

Once authorized, you can immediately browse and play your videos.

Requested permissions

  • files.metadata.read — Browse folders and search videos
  • files.content.read — Stream videos and display thumbnails
  • files.content.write — Rename and delete videos from inside the app
  • account_info.read — Display the signed-in account (name, email, country)

All video playback happens locally on your Mac. No data is uploaded to external servers.

FAQ

Common questions and answers.

What does this app do?

It plays multiple videos from your Dropbox simultaneously and lets you switch layouts while comparing them. Workspaces keep your work state, and batch controls let you drive every clip at once.

Can I download videos directly from a website?

No. This app plays files that are already in Dropbox; it does not download videos from external websites. Please follow the content rights and terms of service for each platform.

Which video formats are supported?

The app is built on Chromium, so it supports a wide range of codecs including H.264, VP9, and AV1. DRM-protected videos cannot be played.

  • Displayed file extensions: .mp4 / .mov / .m4v / .webm / .mkv / .avi / .wmv / .3gp / .ogg
Can I open videos from another app? (for developers)

URL scheme:

dbmvplayer://load?localPath={absolute path to a file inside the Dropbox sync folder}

Parameters:

  • localPath (required): absolute path to a file inside the Dropbox sync folder (URL-encoded)

Swift example:

let path = "/Users/username/Library/CloudStorage/Dropbox/Videos/sample.mp4"
let encoded = path.addingPercentEncoding(withAllowedCharacters: .urlQueryAllowed) ?? ""
let urlString = "dbmvplayer://load?localPath=\(encoded)"
NSWorkspace.shared.open(URL(string: urlString)!)

Shell example:

open "dbmvplayer://load?localPath=/Users/username/Library/CloudStorage/Dropbox/Videos/sample.mp4"
Is a Dropbox account required?

Yes. The app is built around videos stored in Dropbox, so a Dropbox account is required.

Is there a Windows version?

At this time the app is macOS only.
